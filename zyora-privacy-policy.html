ZYORA Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2025

ZYORA (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the ZYORA Chrome Extension.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your name, and email (handled through Firebase Authentication)
  • Generated looks you save using the "Save" button.
  • Number of generated looks per user to monitor model usage.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide a personalized experience, save your looks for later access, and monitor model usage. Your authentication and session are managed securely.

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal data with third party entities.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored locally in your browser or securely on Firebase Storage and Google's Firebase infrastructure to protect your personal data. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information.

5. Your Consent

By using Fitly, you agree to this Privacy Policy and the handling of your data as described above.

6. User Control/Contact

You can sign out at any time and delete your saved looks. To request deletion of your data, contact us at uzma.hmalla@gmail.com.
